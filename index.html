Reduce Reuse Recycle Tips

What Are The 3 Rs?

The 3 Rs help us reduce waste, save natural resources and protect the environment.

🌱

1. REDUCE

Reduce means using fewer resources and creating less waste.

Example: Save water and electricity.

♻️

2. REUSE

Reuse means using things again instead of throwing them away.

Example: Use old jars as pencil holders.

🌳

3. RECYCLE

Recycle means turning waste materials into useful new products.

Example: Recycle paper and plastic.

💚 How Can We Help?

🌿 Plant more trees.

💧 Save water.

💡 Switch off unused lights.

🛍️ Carry reusable shopping bags.

🗑️ Put waste in the correct bins.
